Ridge Roller

Steer a glass marble down floating isometric courses before the clock runs out. Every course is built from a seed, so you can share one. A Marble Madness remake.

How to play

Roll from the start pad to the checkered goal. Time left over carries into the next course, and each course is longer and meaner than the last. You lose time, not lives, when the marble breaks.

  • Drops of more than about two levels shatter the marble; so do the coiled crawlers. Rolling off the edge drops you into the mist.
  • You restart at the last ring you rolled over.
  • Blue tiles are ice, green tiles are goo. Red posts are bumpers. The dark marble tries to shove you off.
