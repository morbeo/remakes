ClockWerx Help

How to play

Your gold clock hand spins around a dot. Grab the next dot just as the tip passes it, and work your way to the sparkling goal dot. Nothing moves until you press a game key, so study the level first.

Swing grabs the dot and spins the other way. Flip grabs it and keeps the same spin. Bounce reverses off the dot without moving. Reverse turns around anywhere. Hold the key before the tip reaches the dot; keep holding to repeat.

With mouse control, click a neighbouring dot while your hand is within a quarter turn of it to swing, double-click to flip, and click your own dot to reverse.

Hit anything dangerous or run out of time and the level restarts, from your way station if you grabbed one.

Scoring

Each level pays a completion bonus by difficulty, points for every second left, and bonus dots worth 1,000, 2,000 and so on up to 5,000 each. Restarting resets the bonus count. In two-player games the first player to the goal gets the full completion bonus, the second half.

About ClockWerx

A fan remake for the browser: one file, no libraries, every graphic drawn in code and every sound synthesised. Levels are original; each one is checked by a solver on all four difficulties before it ships.
