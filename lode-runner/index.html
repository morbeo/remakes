GOLD RAID

A Lode Runner remake: the 1983 look, the sequel's tools and terrain, and online multiplayer.

Click or drag to paint, right-click to erase. Exits: an Exit ladder appears when all gold is taken and leads off the top; an Exit door opens at the same moment.

  • ←→↑↓ or WASD run, climb, drop
  • Z/X or Q/E dig left / right
  • Space use the tool you carry
  • Esc pause, R give up a life when stuck (solo)
  • Two on one keyboard: P1 WASD Q/E F, P2 ←→↑↓ ,/. /
  • Gamepads: stick or d-pad, X/B dig, A tool. Online: 1-4 quick chat
