Sunwell Duel

A Spacewar remake. Two ships, one hungry star.

Controls

RotateA D← →
ThrustW↑
FireS↓
HyperspaceQRight Shift

Solo: either key set flies the Needle, and Space also fires. Esc or P pauses, M mutes. On touch screens use the on-screen pads; in 2-player mode the Wedge's pad sits upside down across the top for a shared tablet.

Thrust burns fuel, which refills while you coast. Torpedoes reload one at a time. Each hyperspace jump is riskier than the last.
