Sunwell Duel

A Spacewar remake. Two armed ships, one hungry star.

Controls

Click a key to change it, then press the new key. Backspace clears a slot, Esc cancels.

Solo: either key set flies the Needle. Esc pauses. On touch screens use the on-screen pads; in 2-player mode the Wedge's pad sits upside down across the top for a shared tablet.

Destroy the other ship with blaster torpedoes or laser pulses until all its shield energy is gone. Torpedoes cost 1 energy and hit for 4; laser pulses cost 1 and hit for 2, and can shoot down incoming torpedoes. Engines, the held shield and the held cloak each burn 1 energy every half second; teleport costs 8. Energy recharges 1 unit every 2 seconds, up to 8 times faster close to the star, and you can move it into the shield or back. Holding the shield makes hits drain energy instead of shield. Touching the star burns 3 shield and flings you away, and a warning beep means it's nearly gone.
