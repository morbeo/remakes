Iron Warren

Drive a tank through a maze that is different every run. Bounce shells off the walls, blow up every enemy hive, then find the hatch down to the next depth. A Bolo (Apple II, 1982) remake with roguelike runs.

How to play
DriveArrows or WASD. Tank steering turns and drives like the original; Direct drives the way you press.
FireSpace, Z or J (hold to keep firing)
Map / pause / muteTab / Esc or P / M
TouchDrag anywhere for a stick under your thumb; tap FIRE
GamepadLeft stick drives, A or a trigger fires, Start pauses

Violet hives build enemy tanks until you destroy them. When every hive on a depth is gone the green hatch opens. Shells ricochet off walls, so you can hit things round corners. Brown walls crack and break. Between depths you pick one of three upgrades and can spend scrap on repairs. Every fifth depth is a hive core. One hull, one life: when it breaks the run is over.
