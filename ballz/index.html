Bead Brawl

A fighting game whose fighters are strung together from beads. Every seed builds a new roster with its own bodies, move sets and arenas.

Controls
1 player2 players: P1 / P2
Move, jump, crouchWASD or arrowsWASD / arrows
PunchJ or ZF / J
KickK or XG / K
SpecialL or CH / L

Hold away from your opponent to block; hold down-away to block low. Every fighter has 13 moves: punch and kick with forward, back or down give different attacks, down + special is a second special, punch + kick together is a throw, and punch or kick in the air is a jump attack. Tap forward or back twice to dash. A move that connects can be cancelled into a stronger one. Sweeps must be blocked low and jump attacks high. The fighter select screen and the pause menu list each fighter's moves. Touch: stick on the left, P K S on the right. Gamepads work too. Esc pauses, M mutes.
