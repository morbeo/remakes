GLINT SHAFT

A Diamond Mine remake: walk the dust off the tunnels, shoot what crawls out of the den, and bank your diamonds before something takes them.

  • ←→↑↓ or WASD walk (turns are buffered)
  • Space, X or J fire the way you face
  • Esc or P pause, M sound
  • Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A/B fire, Start pause
