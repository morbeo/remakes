GLINT SHAFT

A Diamond Mine remake: dig through the shaft, tow the diamonds you find through the processing box and up the lift before the monsters eat them.

  • Player 1: ←→↑↓ or IJKL drive, Space or / fire
  • Player 2: WASD drive, F or 1 fire (solo: these drive player 1 too)
  • Esc or P pause, M sound, G green or colour screen
  • Gamepads: stick or d-pad, A/B fire, Start pause; the second pad drives player 2
