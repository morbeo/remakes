# Diamond Mine: notes on the original

There are two unrelated 8-bit games called Diamond Mine, and sources mix them up.

- **Diamond Mine (Roklan, 1983)**, developed by IDSI for the Apple II, Atari 8-bit and Commodore 64. Top-down single-screen maze game: collect as many diamonds as possible from 20 underground shafts. The shafts start covered in dust and your shots cannot pass through dust until you have walked over it. Monsters are released at intervals from a Pac-Man-style central pen and can be shot. Touching a monster loses all the gems you hold. You can start from one of five screens. This is the game Glint Shaft remakes.
- **Diamond Mine (MRM Software, 1984; Blue Ribbon reissue)** by Mike Williams for the BBC Micro, Electron, Amstrad CPC and others: you steer a pipe through the maze to reach diamonds while monsters patrol. Not this game.

Sources: [MobyGames entry](https://www.mobygames.com/game/30284/), [Retro Replay entry](https://retro-replay.com/db/apple-ii/diamond-mine), [Wikipedia on the MRM game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Mine_(video_game)).

## What the remake keeps and changes

Kept: 20 shafts, dust that blocks shots until walked through, a central monster den that releases monsters on a timer, shootable monsters, losing carried gems on contact, five starting depths.

Changed or added: shafts are generated from a seed instead of fixed layouts (no original layouts are used); carried gems are banked at a lift, with a multiplier for banking several at once; lost gems spill nearby instead of vanishing; lamps as lives; three monster kinds including one that re-dusts tunnels; daily seed, shareable seed links, buffered turns, touch, swipe and gamepad controls, local best runs.
